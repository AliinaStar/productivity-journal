# Privacy Policy for Litopys

**Last updated: 24 July 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how the **Litopys** mobile application ("Litopys",
"the app", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information. Litopys is
a personal productivity journal that lets you record entries and goals and
generates summary reports.

By using Litopys you agree to the practices described in this policy.

---

## 1. Who we are

Litopys is developed and operated by an individual developer.
For any privacy-related questions you can contact us at:

**Email:** mylitopys@gmail.com

---

## 2. What data we collect

We collect only the data needed to provide the app's features:

- **Account data** — your email address (used to sign you in).
- **Profile** — the name you enter, your chosen report language, and your age
  range. You can optionally add your gender, which is used to personalise your
  reports.
- **Content you create** — your goals (title, description, deadline, status) and
  your journal entries (the note text, the date the entry is about, and a
  productivity score from 1 to 5), plus the reports generated from them.
- **Search index** — to let you find past notes, we compute and store a numeric
  vector (embedding) of each note's text on our own servers. No third party is
  involved in this.
- **Preferences** — your chosen interface language (stored on your device).
- **Authentication data** — temporary one-time login codes (stored only as a
  hashed value) and session tokens that keep you signed in.
- **Consent record** — the date and time you accepted this policy.
- **Push notification token** — if you allow notifications, a device token used
  to send reminders. You can revoke it at any time in your device settings.

We do **not** collect your precise location, contacts, photos, advertising
identifiers, or any data we do not need to run the app.

---

## 3. How we use your data

We use your data only to:

- sign you in and keep your session secure;
- store and display your journal entries, goals, and reports;
- generate summary reports from the entries you choose to include;
- send you one-time login codes by email.

We do **not** sell your data, and we do **not** use it for advertising.

---

## 4. Third-party services

To provide certain features, some data is processed by trusted third parties:

- **OpenAI** — when you generate a report, the relevant journal content is sent
  to OpenAI's API to produce the summary. See OpenAI's privacy policy at
  https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
- **Resend** — your email address is used to deliver one-time login codes. See
  https://resend.com/legal/privacy-policy
- **Hosting (netcup, Germany/EU)** — our backend and database are hosted on
  servers located in the European Union.

These providers process data only on our behalf and for the purposes above.

---

## 5. How your data is stored and protected

- All communication between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit
  using HTTPS/TLS.
- Login codes are never stored in plain text — only a hashed value is kept, and
  codes expire after a short time.
- Access to your account is protected by signed session tokens.
- Our database is hosted on access-controlled servers in the EU.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable
measures to protect your information.

---

## 6. Data retention and deletion

- We keep your account data and content for as long as your account exists.
- You can **export** your data from within the app at any time.
- You can **delete your account and all associated data directly in the app**
  (Profile → Delete account). This immediately and permanently removes your
  account and all goals, notes, and reports, and cannot be undone.
- If you prefer, you can also request deletion by emailing us at
  mylitopys@gmail.com, and we will delete your data within a reasonable period
  after your request.

---

## 7. Children's privacy

Litopys is intended for people aged 13 and older. When you sign up you select an
age range, and no under-13 option is offered, so we do not knowingly collect
data from children. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us data,
contact us and we will remove it.

---

## 8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at
the top reflects the latest version. Significant changes will be communicated
within the app or by email where appropriate.
