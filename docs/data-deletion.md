# Data Deletion for Litopys

**Last updated: 25 July 2026**

Litopys is developed and operated by an individual developer. For any
questions about this process, contact us at:

**Email:** mylitopys@gmail.com

You can delete parts of your data without deleting your account, or delete
your account entirely.

---

## Delete a goal and its entries (partial deletion)

1. Open the **Litopys** app and sign in.
2. Open the goal you want to remove.
3. Tap **Edit**, then tap **Delete** at the bottom of the screen.
4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

This immediately and permanently removes the goal and every journal entry
linked to it. Your account, profile, and other goals are not affected.

---

## Delete everything (full account deletion)

Deleting your account removes all of your data at once — see
[Account Deletion](/account-deletion/) for the steps and full list of what is
removed.

---

## Data retention

Deleted goals, entries, and accounts are removed immediately — there is no
grace period, and no copy is kept for backups, analytics, or any other
purpose beyond standard infrastructure backup rotation.

---

## Alternative: request deletion without app access

If you no longer have access to the app, email us at
**mylitopys@gmail.com** from the address registered to your account and
specify what you want deleted (a specific goal, or your entire account). We
will act on the request within 30 days.
