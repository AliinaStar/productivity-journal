# Account Deletion for Litopys

**Last updated: 25 July 2026**

Litopys is developed and operated by an individual developer. For any
questions about this process, contact us at:

**Email:** mylitopys@gmail.com

---

## How to delete your account

1. Open the **Litopys** app and sign in.
2. Go to the **Profile** tab.
3. Scroll down and tap **Delete account** (Видалити акаунт).
4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

Your account is deleted immediately — there is no waiting period and no need
to contact support.

---

## What data is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes, right away:

- Your profile (email, name, language, gender, age range)
- All goals you created
- All journal entries linked to those goals
- All generated reports
- Your authentication data (login codes, refresh tokens/sessions)

No data is retained after deletion — nothing is kept in backups beyond our
standard infrastructure backup rotation, and no copy is kept for analytics or
any other purpose.

---

## Alternative: request deletion without app access

If you no longer have access to the app (e.g. you lost your device), email us
at **mylitopys@gmail.com** from the address registered to your account and
request deletion. We will delete your account and all associated data within
30 days.
